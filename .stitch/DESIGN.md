# Expandi Universal Design System

## Brand & Visual Philosophy
The design language is engineered for high-growth modern B2B SaaS, characterized by crisp, ultra-clean white canvases punctuated by high-contrast Royal Electric Blue (`#001AD3`), Sky Blue (`#21ABE1`), and energetic multi-pastel bento containers (Soft Lemon `#FFF480`, Ice Blue `#E0F3FB`, Lilac `#D2C5FA`, and Peach `#FECEBF`).

It creates an immediate impression of speed, clarity, safety, and high-velocity conversion, replacing cluttered dark patterns with crisp, human-approachable structure.

## Core Visual Signatures
1. **Pill Badges & Highlights**: Micro-badges with icon + text on soft tinted pastel pills (`rounded-full`, `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`).
2. **Text Mark Accents**: `<mark>` elements or bold highlighted words using custom accent backgrounds.
3. **Multi-Pastel Bento Containers**: High-radius cards (`rounded-xl` / `20px`) with distinct pastel color themes per card.
4. **Two-Direction Motion**: Continuous scrolling marquees moving in opposing directions for feature discovery and social proof.
5. **Bold High-Impact Metric Banners**: Deep royal blue or electric sky blue full-width cards with large white numeric callouts and divider hairlines.
6. **Polished Micro-Interactions**: Hover lifts (`translateY(-2px)`), glow outlines, button scale springs (`scale(0.98)` on active), and active card color inversions.
